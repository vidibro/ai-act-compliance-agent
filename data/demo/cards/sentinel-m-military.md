## Scope and status

- **Where it is used:** supplied to and used by armed forces in the Union.
- **Exclusions claimed:** Art. 2(3). Sentinel-M is placed on the market and used exclusively for

  units in the field. The provider offers it for civil, law-enforcement or dual use in no form; the
  contracts forbid any such use and the software is bound to the vehicles' military identification
  module.
- **Part of a regulated product:** none of the Annex I instruments applies to military vehicles.
- **Placed on the market:** version 1.0 in 2021; version 4.0 in March 2026.

## Use case 1: threat detection for armoured vehicles

**Intended purpose.** Detecting and classifying incoming projectiles and hostile drones from radar
and infrared sensors on an armoured vehicle, and cueing the vehicle's countermeasure system.
Affects: vehicle crews.

**How outputs are produced.** A convolutional neural network trained on recorded and simulated
sensor tracks classifies each track; a rules layer decides whether to cue a countermeasure.

**What it does to a person.** Nothing about any civilian; it classifies objects in the air.

**Human involvement.** The commander arms and disarms the system; in automatic mode the system
cues countermeasures without a confirmation step because the engagement window is under a second.

**Content generation / interaction.** None.

## Out of scope

Not offered for border surveillance, policing, critical infrastructure protection or any civil
purpose.
