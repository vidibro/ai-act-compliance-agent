## Use case 1: shopping assistant chat

**Intended purpose.** Answering shoppers' questions about products, delivery and returns in a chat
window on the retailer's web shop. Affects: shoppers.

**How outputs are produced.** The assistant is built on a licensed third-party general-purpose AI
model, with retrieval over the retailer's catalogue and policies. It is an AI system built on a
model (Art. 3(66)), and this card describes the system.

**What it does to a person.** It answers questions. It holds no profile of the shopper and makes no
decision about them; the same question gets the same answer whoever asks.

**What it does not do.** No pricing decisions, no eligibility decisions, no personalised offers, no
identity or age inference, no emotion recognition.

**Content generation / interaction.** The chatbot interacts directly with shoppers, in natural
language, by text.

## Use case 2: product description drafting

**Intended purpose.** Generating draft product descriptions for the retailer's catalogue from a
structured product sheet. Affects: nobody directly; the text is published on the web shop after the
retailer's review.

**How outputs are produced.** The same model generates text from the product sheet and a style
guide.

**What it does to a person.** Nothing. No personal data is used; no person is evaluated.

**Content generation / interaction.** The system generates text that is published to the public.

## Transparency in the chat — Art. 50(1)

Every conversation opens with the assistant's own message: "Hi, I'm ShopAssist, an automated
assistant. I'm not a person. Ask me about products, delivery or returns, or type 'agent' at any time
to reach a human." The header of the chat window carries the persistent label "Automated
assistant" for the whole conversation, and the label is read out by screen readers. The disclosure
is repeated in plain language if the shopper asks whether they are talking to a person. The
retailer cannot remove or rename the disclosure; it is part of the widget. Where a retailer's shop
is aimed at children, the opening message is shortened to one sentence and shown with an icon.

## Marking of generated text — Art. 50(2)

Generated product descriptions are published with the footer "Written with AI assistance" on the
product page. We consider this sufficient marking of AI-generated content.

## Out of scope

Not intended for medical, financial or legal advice; the assistant declines such questions and
points to a human agent.
