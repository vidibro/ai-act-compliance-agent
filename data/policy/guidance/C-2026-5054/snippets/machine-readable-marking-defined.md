(71) The scope of the marking obligation is limited to implementing marks in a machine-readable format. A machine-readable format means that marks are structured in a way that allows software applications to easily identify, recognise and extract them without human intervention. Perceptible marks and labels are not excluded as a complementary measure, where appropriate, with a view to facilitating the compliance of deployers with their obligation to label deep fakes pursuant to Article 50(4) AI Act.
