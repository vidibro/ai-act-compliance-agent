(336) In some Member States, the search for a missing person may occur under an administrative procedure and not for law enforcement purposes. For example, where a vulnerable person is missing, but there is no suspicion of a crime or any other law enforcement purpose, the use of real-time RBI systems to search for that person would not be deemed to be for law enforcement purposes and would therefore fall under the rules for such use under the GDPR.
