# Interview Intelligence — how it works

*Anonymised from a real public vendor page (July 2026). Rewritten, not copied. Not a labelled case.*

## Interview every applicant, not just the shortlist

Most teams can only interview the few candidates who made it past a CV screen. Interview
Intelligence removes that ceiling. Every applicant records answers to your questions on their own
schedule, and our AI reviews all of them — so a great candidate with an average CV still gets seen.

## What the AI reviews

Our models were trained on millions of interview responses and evaluate each recording across
several dimensions:

- **What is said** — relevance to the question, clarity of reasoning, structure of the answer, and
  correct use of the vocabulary of the role.
- **How it is said** — pace, tone of voice and vocal energy, which our research associates with
  confidence and engagement.
- **Visual signals** — facial expression, gaze and body language, used to gauge enthusiasm and
  composure under pressure.
- **Integrity** — eye movement, background audio and on-screen activity are monitored to flag
  possible assistance or misrepresentation during the session.

## Scorecards your team can act on

Every completed interview produces a scorecard with an overall rating and a strengths-and-weaknesses
breakdown. Candidates are ranked so your hiring manager sees the strongest first. Predictive
performance scoring highlights those most likely to succeed in the role based on patterns from
previous successful hires at similar companies.

## Fully automated, always fair

The AI conducts and reviews interviews without human intervention, handling up to 80% of your
recruitment cycle. It applies exactly the same criteria to every candidate, every time — no tired
interviewer at 5pm, no unconscious bias, no inconsistency between panels.

Our models act like your best human recruiters, only faster and at any scale. Hiring managers
always retain final say over who is hired.

## Outcomes

Teams using Interview Intelligence report 70% faster time-to-hire and a measurable improvement in
first-year retention among AI-recommended hires.
