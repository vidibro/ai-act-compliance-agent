# Smart Screening — product overview

*Anonymised from a real public vendor page (July 2026). Rewritten, not copied. Not a labelled case.*

## Stop drowning in applications

Hiring teams receive hundreds of applications per opening and read a fraction of them. Smart
Screening reads every single one, so nobody good gets lost in the pile.

Our platform understands what a role actually needs — not just keywords. It reads each application
in context, understands the intent behind how experience is described, and connects it to your
requirements. Recruiters get a clear starting point instead of an inbox.

## How it works

Applications arrive through your careers page and job boards and are processed automatically within
seconds. Documents in any common format are handled, including scans.

## Features

**Instant parsing.** Contact details, work history, education, certifications and skills are pulled
into structured fields and written straight to your ATS. No more copy-paste.

**Contextual matching.** Requirements from the job description are compared against what each
applicant actually did, not just the words they used.

**Fit scoring.** Every applicant receives a match score against the role, weighted across required
skills, preferred experience, seniority and any criteria your team adds. Scores update automatically
when the requisition changes.

**Ranked shortlists.** Applicants are ordered by score so your recruiters start with the strongest
10–20% and work down. Shortlists sync back to your ATS.

**Bias controls.** Names, photographs, addresses and dates of birth can be hidden from reviewers.

**Explanations.** Each score comes with the factors that drove it, so your team can see the
reasoning.

## Humans stay in charge

Smart Screening does not make hiring decisions. It organises the work so recruiters can spend their
time on the judgement calls that matter — motivation, culture, potential — instead of triage. Your
team decides who moves forward, every time.

## Results

Customers report a 75% reduction in time-to-shortlist and a 90% improvement in recruiter
productivity. One enterprise customer reduced screening effort by more than 1,000 hours per quarter.
