# Coordination Cloud — interview scheduling, automated

*Anonymised from a real public vendor page (July 2026). Rewritten, not copied. Not a labelled case.*

## Give your coordinators their week back

Scheduling interviews is the least valuable and most time-consuming part of hiring. Twelve emails to
find a slot. A panel that falls apart when one interviewer declines. A candidate in another time
zone. Coordination Cloud handles all of it.

## Scheduling that runs itself

When a candidate reaches the interview stage, Coordination Cloud reads live calendar availability
across your interviewer pool, proposes times that work for everyone, and confirms the booking. The
candidate picks from a self-service link or replies over SMS or WhatsApp. Confirmations, calendar
invites, video links, directions and reminders all go out automatically.

**Panel and multi-day loops.** Sequential interviews, panels and full onsite days are assembled in
one pass, with breaks and room bookings included.

**Interviewer matching.** The system selects interviewers for each session based on skill coverage,
current interview load, time zone and whether they have completed your interviewer training. Load is
balanced across the pool so the same people are not asked every time.

**Self-healing changes.** When an interviewer declines, a replacement with equivalent coverage is
found and the loop is rebuilt without anyone chasing. Candidates reschedule themselves.

**Works where you work.** Native integrations with Google and Microsoft calendars and the major
applicant tracking systems. Scheduling status writes back to the candidate record.

## Privacy by design

No personally identifiable information passes through our AI systems. Scheduling operates on
anonymised availability and coverage data only.

Customers schedule roughly ten times faster than with manual coordination, cut reschedules by half,
and save around three months of coordinator time per recruiter per year.
