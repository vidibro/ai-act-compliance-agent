# Mandatory-content audit register

**Snapshot:** 2026-07-27; **baseline:** `0951522`; **reviewer:** codex; **date:** 2026-09-02.

This second pass compared all **146 norms with nonempty `mandatory_content`** against their linked
source text, including introductory and qualifier spans. It complements the
[88-segment granularity triage](NORMATIVE-COVERAGE-GRANULARITY-AUDIT.md), not the full independent
review of all 1,347 norms.

Results: **19 additional split recommendations, 12 expansions, 38 references to
existing findings/decisions, and 77 records with no additional finding in this focused pass.**
These are norm-level rows, not segment counts or numbers of new norms; do not add them to the
segment-ledger counts as though the units were the same.

The important distinction is between document contents, alternative eligibility/decision criteria,
illustrative topics, enumerated powers and independently triggered actions. All currently use the
same list field. Runtime compilation must preserve their different logic, not apply a universal
AND-of-mandatory-checks interpretation. Exact source links preserve evidence but do not, by
themselves, supply that executable meaning.

“No additional finding” is deliberately narrow: it is not legal approval, implementation coverage,
or a conclusion that all other norm fields are correct. Referenced annex-content norms are not
missing simply because their parent article cites an annex instead of duplicating its checklist.
In Annex XIII, the 10,000-business-user presumption is already a separate linked norm; the shorter
parent criterion therefore does not establish an omission.

No legal inventory or review status was changed by this audit. Corrections are proposed in source
order; norms and unflagged sources outside this focused pass remain subject to independent review.
